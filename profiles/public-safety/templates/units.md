UNITS BY COUNTY
- FIRE UNIT NUMBERING (how a spoken unit maps to a station or department), per county:
  - <County>: e.g. station number first ("96 Engine 1" = Station 96), or station digits in the unit ("Engine 206" = Station 200), or department name first ("Middle Creek Engine 1").
- All counties: fire and EMS units are MED, MEDIC, ENGINE, TRUCK, LADDER, SQUAD, RESCUE, TANKER, BRUSH, CHIEF, BATTALION, STATION or COMMAND plus a number. Transcripts mishear them ("MADDEN 1", "MAN 1" = MED 1).
- <County>: law unit numbering (sheriff series, police call signs), CORONER unit series (used by RULES.md SERIOUS), supervisor unit names and how transcripts mishear them.
